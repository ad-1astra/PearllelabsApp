Launch Lab needs to run on your own computer -- it installs software, reads your USB ports, and prompts for sudo, none of which a hosted page can do on your behalf. This page is just the entry point.

1. Open a terminal on the machine your robot is plugged into and run:

uv run lerobot-launch-lab

2. Then click below to open it:

Open Launch Lab on this computer

If nothing happens, the local helper isn't running yet, or it's on a different port -- add ?host=127.0.0.1:PORT to this page's URL.
